Business rule: which revenue counts

  • Total item sales counts every order line in view, whatever its status. It measures what customers ordered.
  • Completed-order revenue counts only COMPLETE orders. This is the figure to treat as realised revenue.
  • CANCELLED orders were not fulfilled and REFUNDED orders were paid back, so neither is realised revenue. They are shown separately, never silently dropped.
  • The data holds only COMPLETE, CANCELLED and REFUNDED. The schema also allows OPEN, PAID and SHIPPED, which do not occur, so no rule is needed for them. If they appear in future data they would show up in the status filter and be excluded from completed-order revenue.
  • Use the status chips to see any slice. Orders are always counted once by order_id.

KPI formulas

  • Line revenue = unit_price × quantity (price actually charged on the order line).
  • Total item sales = Σ line revenue.
  • Total orders = count of distinct order_id, not item rows.
  • Average order value = total item sales ÷ total orders.
  • Average items per order = total quantity ÷ total orders. Order lines per order: .
  • Completed-order revenue = Σ line revenue where status = COMPLETE.

Data quality checks

  • Duplicates: none. 0 repeated order_id, 0 repeated (order_id, line_item_id), 0 fully duplicated rows.
  • Nulls: none in orders or order lines, except shipment_id (1,238 of 3,914 lines), which is allowed to be empty. It is not used here.
  • Order statuses: only COMPLETE (1,892), CANCELLED (35), REFUNDED (23). None unexpected.
  • Joins: 0 orphan lines. Every order line matches an order, product and store.
  • Ranges: quantity 1 to 5; unit price 5.65 to 49.12; orders 4 Feb 2021 to 12 Apr 2022.
  • Store names: shown as stored, including "Bejing" and "New Dehli".
Product price caveat. For 3,841 of 3,914 lines, the order-line price does not match the price of the product it points to. Those prices equal the price of the product with ID one lower. Revenue uses the price charged, so totals, stores, months and statuses are unaffected. Product-level charts follow the product_id on each line and could shift by one product if the sample data is misaligned. Check the source before relying on them.

Assumptions and limits

  • Revenue is item value only. There is no tax, shipping, discount or currency field.
  • Orders have no stored total, so every figure is derived from order lines.
  • The Online store accounts for most sales, so store comparisons are lopsided.
  • Dates use the date part of order_ts.
  • Validate by re-running the SQL: SELECT SUM(unit_price*quantity), COUNT(DISTINCT order_id), SUM(quantity) FROM order_items JOIN orders USING (order_id). Expected unfiltered: 308,598.53 / 1,950 / 11,827.
